1. Problem & goals

Exatech runs three branches on a mix of spreadsheets, a CRM, a fee register and WhatsApp groups. Admissions are re-keyed three times, fee clearance is checked by hand before notes are shared, teachers mark attendance on paper, and parents hear about absences a week late. Nothing is auditable, and management has no live view across branches.

Goals
  1. Zero re-keying. A lead becomes an admission becomes a student, an enrollment, an invoice schedule and two portal accounts in one approved flow.
  2. Fees drive access. Fee clearance is computed, cached on the enrollment and recomputed on every payment; it gates learning material instantly.
  3. Teachers work from a phone. Attendance, marks, assignments and notes in under three taps, including sessions that are not on the timetable.
  4. Parents hear the same day. Absence, fee-due, exam and marks alerts on WhatsApp with no manual step.
  5. Everything is explainable. Every money-moving or grade-moving action is logged with actor, role, before/after and reason.

Non-goals (v1)

  • Payroll and HR beyond leave and substitutes · LMS authoring (quizzes, SCORM) · Placement management · Hostel/transport · Multi-institution SaaS tenancy (single institution, many branches).

3. Information architecture

Institution → Branch → Course offering → Batch → Enrollment. Courses are defined once at institution level and offered per branch with a local fee plan; batches carry schedule, room, teacher-per-subject and capacity. A teacher's assignment is (teacher, batch) → subject, so one teacher teaches Search in DM-A and Paid media in DM-B.

4. Identity & shell

  • Invite-only sign-in — Google, email magic link or phone OTP. Accounts are created by admission approval (student + parent) or staff invite (role × scope applied). No self-registration.
  • Sessions — staff 12 h with 30-min idle timeout; student/parent 7-day rolling. 2FA mandatory for Super Admin and Finance.
  • Shell — branch switcher, ⌘K command palette (students, leads, invoices, batches, teachers, branches, actions), live notification bell with count, account menu with membership switch and sign-out, grouped sidebar that collapses behind a menu button under 900 px.
  • Detail panels — any student, teacher/staff member, branch or exam opens in a right-side drawer with its complete record and contextual actions (see §5–§9). Deep links everywhere: names in tables, "Open" on at-risk rows, palette results.
  • Impersonation — Super Admin "View as student": read-only, time-boxed to 15 minutes, logged with reason.

6. Branch Administrator

Branch operations

Sessions today (incl. ad-hoc sessions awaiting acknowledgement), running batches, admissions pending, marks to publish, at-risk students (attendance < 75 %, overdue dues, missed work — each opens the student panel).

Courses & batches

Course offerings per branch (duration, mode, fee-plan default) and batches with schedule, teacher · subject (click to open teacher), room, live seat utilisation (updates on admission approval), fee plan and status (planned / open / running / waitlist). Filters: all / running / open / planned.

Timetable

  • Week grid by room / teacher / batch; chips show batch · room · teacher · subject; click a chip to edit.
  • Add / edit session dialog: batch (auto-fills teacher and subject from the assignment), teacher, subject (editable), day, 2-hour slot, room, mode. Save is blocked with reasons for: room clash, teacher clash, cross-branch teacher on-site days, approved leave. Remove session supported. Every change notifies the batch and is logged.
  • Conflicts panel is live and offers one-click fixes ("Move UX-A to Room 4"). Validations listed: teacher availability across branches (45-min buffer), room capacity ≥ seats, holidays suppress sessions.
  • Substitute: when a teacher is on leave the affected session is flagged; assigning a substitute updates the chip, notifies students and both teachers, and re-runs clash checks (picking a busy teacher surfaces a new conflict).

Admissions

Queue of applications showing batch & seats, salesperson, fee plan, discount, three-step progress (Sales → Finance → Branch Admin), documents. Approve is disabled until Finance has verified fee terms and, above 20 % discount, until Super Admin has approved. On approval the system issues the admission number, creates the enrollment and invoice schedule, invites student and parent, marks the lead Won, increments the batch seat count and sends the welcome WhatsApp with the first invoice.

Students

Searchable list (name, phone, admission number, barcode scan) with batch, guardian, attendance, fee status, enrollment state; PII masked in lists; exports watermarked. Go-live import and ID-card printing. The student panel shows attendance, fee balance and next due, published marks, pending work, profile facts, fee account rows, recent attendance, documents and a timeline; actions: Collect payment (opens the desk pre-loaded), Timetable, Transfer batch (target with seats, effective date, reason, pro-rated adjustment), Print ID card, View as student.

Notes & material (oversight)

All material across batches with module, type, visibility (always / fee-gated / scheduled), uploader and open rate; counts of fee-gated items and students currently locked out (linked to Dues).

Marks publishing

Exams by batch with teacher, submission time, student count, average and status. For submitted exams: Review grid (average, highest, lowest, missing rows, full read-only grid), Publish (students and parents notified immediately) or Return to the teacher with a note. Draft marks are never visible to students or parents.

Announcements

Title, rich message, audience (batch, role, segment such as overdue fees, branch, course), schedule and expiry, pin, acknowledgement required, also-WhatsApp. Published items appear instantly on student and parent home and show read/ack counts.

7. Sales & Sales Manager

Pipeline & leads

Kanban across seven stages (New → Contacted → Counselled → Demo scheduled → Negotiation → Admission in progress → Won) with lead score, source and owner; one-click advance; table view with saved filters and a lead record (consent flags, activity timeline, next follow-up, Call / WhatsApp / Email). New lead form validates name and 10-digit phone, dedupes on phone/email, assigns by rule and starts the first-response timer.

Follow-ups & templates

Overdue and Today queues with log-outcome; Meta-approved WhatsApp templates with variables; personal month stats vs target.

Import

Four steps — upload (CSV/XLSX ≤ 10,000 rows), map & preview, validate & dedupe (merge / skip / create), run as a background job with downloadable error report and round-robin assignment.

Convert to admission

Five steps prefilled from the lead: student details, guardian (parent login via OTP), batch with live seats and waitlist, fee plan & discount request (threshold warnings), documents & DPDP consent. Submitting places the application in the Finance verification queue and moves the lead to Admission in progress.

Sales Manager

  • Funnel with step-to-step conversion, lost reasons and by-source table; median first response.
  • Assignment rules evaluated top-down (source, course, preferred branch, fallback round-robin) and bulk reassignment (e.g. staff on leave).
  • Targets & leaderboard: leads, contacted < 2 h, admissions vs target, attributed revenue.

8. Finance

Dashboard

Collected today, expected this week, overdue (students, promises), pending clearance (cheques, online), fee verifications; today by method; dues ageing; collection vs target by branch. The Fee verification queue is step 2 of admission: Finance verifies plan, discount and documents, or returns to Sales.

Collection desk

  • Scan the admission barcode or search by name / phone / number; the fee account loads with instalments, paid, balance and the suggested next-due amount.
  • Amount (minimum partial ₹1,000), method (UPI, cash, card, cheque, transfer) with method-specific reference, split tender, UPI QR, keyboard-first (⏎ confirm).
  • Confirm prints the receipt (A4 / A5 / 80 mm), sends WhatsApp + email, allocates oldest-due-first, marks the invoice Paid or Partially paid, recomputes fee clearance and unlocks notes. Cheques stay pending clearance and do not unlock. Excess is held as credit.

Student fee account

Totals, overdue with late fee, clearance status with temporary exemption (reason required), append-only ledger (reversals, never deletes), instalment schedule with revisions, restructure (approval above ₹10,000), follow-up log, promise-to-pay, reminders and payment link.

Invoices & receipts · Dues · Day book · Reconciliation · Settings

  • Gap-free numbering per branch per FY; cancel issues a credit note; reprints watermarked "Duplicate" and logged; bulk generation per batch; verification QR on every document.
  • Dues with ageing buckets, last reminder, promise; one-click reminders (stop automatically on payment); announce to the overdue segment.
  • Day book per counter with denominations, difference and explanation; closing locks the day; period locks need Super Admin to reopen; maker-checker for discounts, waivers, refunds, voids, restructures.
  • Razorpay settlement auto-match by payment id + amount; unmatched → record as advance / investigate; idempotent webhooks retried 72 h.
  • Settings: numbering, GST rate & SAC, grace and late fee with cap, approval thresholds, methods & allocation order, document templates.

9. Teacher

Mobile-first; every action completes in ≤ 3 taps; works offline for attendance and syncs later.

Today

Sessions with live marked counts, the next session's Mark attendance (routes to that batch), ad-hoc session card, "Cover another class", to-grade count across batches, missing-marks count across draft exams, leave list with Request leave (shows which session will need a substitute; pending until the Branch Admin approves).

Attendance

  • Batch · session picker (own batches) and Subject taught picker (defaults to the assigned subject; any syllabus subject, Doubt-clearing, Revision, Guest lecture). A different subject tags the session, informs the subject teacher and updates the syllabus log.
  • Cover another class: any batch in the branch, type (substitute / extra / doubt-clearing / guest), start, mandatory reason → roster loads; the session is flagged ad-hoc in Branch operations and counts after acknowledgement; regular teacher notified.
  • Tap to cycle Present → Absent → Late → Excused; Mark all present; QR self check-in (rotating 5-minute code, Wi-Fi/geo-fenced); Submit stamps time, sends same-day absence alerts to parents, logs the counts; edits allowed 48 h, later changes need admin override with reason.

Marks

  • Exam picker across the teacher's batches (status in label) and New exam: batch, name (duplicate check), date, up to four components with max marks; roster rows created; appears on students' timetable.
  • Grid with per-component inputs; over-max cells highlighted and totals show "!"; partial totals shown with "…"; Fill empty with 0; CSV import by admission number.
  • Submit only when every row is complete and valid; Recall while awaiting publish; published exams are read-only (corrections via Branch Admin override with reason).

Assignments

All / per-batch filter; cards show due, max, submitted/graded progress, late policy. New assignment (title, batch, due, max, late policy, rubric). Grade dialog lists ungraded submissions with on-time/late flag, marks (over-max blocked) and optional feedback; partial saves keep the rest queued; releasing updates the student's Work tab. Remind non-submitters; extend due date (batch notified).

Notes & material

Per-batch view by module; Upload (batch, module, title, type, visibility always / fee-gated / scheduled-with-date); tap a visibility tag to cycle; remove. Files via 5-minute signed URLs, PDFs watermarked with the student's name.

12. Business rules

  1. Admission approval order: Sales submits → Finance verifies fee terms → (Super Admin approves if discount > 20 %) → Branch Admin approves. Each gate blocks the next.
  2. Capacity is enforced at approval; overflow goes to the waitlist; seat hold 48 h after booking payment.
  3. Fee clearance policy (default: current instalment paid) is per course, cached on the enrollment and recomputed on payment webhook, void, waiver or exemption. Cheques count only after clearance.
  4. Partial payments allowed above ₹1,000; allocation oldest-due-first (or component priority); late fee per day on unpaid balance after grace, capped.
  5. Marks are two-step: teacher submits (complete and valid only) → Branch Admin publishes or returns. Drafts are invisible to students and parents.
  6. Attendance edits by the teacher within 48 h; afterwards admin override with reason. Ad-hoc sessions count after Branch Admin acknowledgement.
  7. Timetable saves are rejected on room or teacher clash, on-site rule violations and approved leave; holidays suppress sessions and pause fee reminders.
  8. Reminders stop automatically when a payment posts. Reprints are watermarked and logged. Closed periods are read-only without Super Admin approval.
  9. A teacher's subject per batch is the default for sessions and exams but any session may be tagged with another syllabus subject; the subject teacher is informed.

16. Engineering standards

  • Feature-based architecture. apps/web/src/features/<feature>/{components,hooks,api,schemas,index.ts} and apps/api/src/modules/<module>/{controllers,services,repositories,events,dto}. A feature exposes a public index.ts; cross-feature imports go through it only.
  • SOLID. Single responsibility per handler/hook; open for extension via variants, adapters and event subscribers rather than flags; interfaces for every external provider (payments, messaging, storage); repositories hide the ORM; thin controllers, fat domain services.
  • Scalability. Stateless web and API (horizontal scale), work queued to BullMQ, read models for dashboards, keyset pagination, cache-and-invalidate rather than cache-and-expire for money and marks.
  • Performance first. Server components by default; client components opt-in; route-level code splitting; images via next/image; fonts self-hosted; no layout shift (skeletons sized to content); heavy tables virtualised above 200 rows.
  • Quality gates. 100 % typed (no any), ESLint strict + import boundaries, unit tests for domain rules (§12), contract tests for every Zod schema, Playwright flows for the admission chain, payment → unlock, and marks publish.
  • Accessibility. Radix primitives, focus management in drawers/dialogs, 4.5:1 contrast, keyboard-first collection desk.
